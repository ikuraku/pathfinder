About us

Helping students find their way

PathFinder is a career guidance platform built by a team of three students. Our goal is simple: help every student understand what they are good at, show it with real proof, and find the opportunities that fit them.

We combine your profile, your tested skills and your preferences to guide you towards internships and careers that make sense for you.
